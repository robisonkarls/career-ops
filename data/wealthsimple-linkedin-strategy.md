## Decision: Reach Out Now?

**YES, if:**
- You want to warm up the conversation before applying
- You found a hiring manager who seems like someone you'd enjoy working with
- You have something specific to reference (their article, open-source project, etc.)

**NO, if:**
- You prefer to let the application speak for itself
- You want to wait 3-5 days after applying to gauge if you get an interview callback first

**My recommendation:** Draft the connection requests now (don't send), then send them **the day after you submit the application**. That gives you momentum and shows genuine interest.

## KEY RULE FOR WEALTHSIMPLE OUTREACH

**Be genuine.** Wealthsimple's culture values authenticity and engineering excellence. Don't send form messages. Every connection request should:
- ✅ Reference something specific (fintech experience, agentic AI, regulatory complexity)
- ✅ Show you understand their role (not just "I want a job")

- ❌ No "I'm interested in your company" generic nonsense
- ❌ No emojis or overenthusiasm
